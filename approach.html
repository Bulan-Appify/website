Delivery

Five stages.

You always know what is happening, what it costs, and what you receive at the end of each stage. If a stage is not delivering value, you can stop at the end of it without penalty.

STAGE 01

Conversation

Thirty minutes with an engineer, free. We want to understand the problem, the constraints and what "done" looks like to you. If we are not the right firm, we say so on that call and point you at someone better suited — a referral costs us nothing and a bad engagement costs us our reputation.

Free30 minutesNo pitch deck

STAGE 02

Discovery

A fixed-fee, time-boxed investigation — typically two weeks at R45 000. We map requirements, constraints, integrations, data and risks, then produce a solution architecture, a delivery plan and a costed estimate with the assumptions written down.

The output is yours, outright, whatever you do next. Take it to another supplier if you want to; several will quote more accurately because of it. We would rather be judged on the quality of that document than on how well we can defend a guess.

R45 000 fixed1–2 weeksYou own the output

STAGE 03

Delivery

Two-week iterations, each ending with something you can actually use. A shared board you can open at any time, a weekly written update, and quality, security and accessibility checks running inside the pipeline rather than waiting for a phase at the end.

Scope changes are expected. New work goes on the backlog with an estimate attached and you decide what comes off to make room, or you extend. No change-request theatre.

2-week sprintsWorking demo each sprintShared boardWeekly written update

STAGE 04

Verification

Independent test execution, security review and a performance baseline before anything reaches production. You receive an evidence pack — test results, defect register, security findings, remediation status and the residual risks stated plainly — and a go / no-go recommendation.

We will recommend against release when that is the honest answer, and we will tell you exactly what would have to change for the answer to be different.

Evidence packResidual risk statedGo / no-go

STAGE 05

Handover or care

Documentation, runbooks, architecture decision records, an environment and secrets inventory, and a recorded working session with your team. Then either we step away cleanly, or you take a care plan and we keep the system patched, monitored and healthy.

RunbooksRecorded handover30-day warrantyOptional care plan

Engagement models

Four ways to buy from us.

We will recommend the one that is genuinely cheaper for you, even where it is less lucrative for us — mostly because the alternative is an unhappy client and a difficult final invoice.

MODEL 01

Fixed-scope project

A defined deliverable for a defined price, with milestone payments.

Best when: scope is genuinely knowable — a penetration test, an assessment, a well-understood build after discovery.

Watch out for: anyone offering a fixed price on a complex build before discovery. They are either padding heavily or planning to recover the margin through change requests.

40% deposit · balance on milestones

MODEL 02

Dedicated squad

A cross-functional team working your backlog on a monthly retainer.

Best when: you have an evolving product, a long roadmap, or a direction that will legitimately change as you learn things.

Why it is often cheaper: you are not paying a risk premium, and nobody on either side is incentivised to argue about whether something was in scope.

Monthly in advance · 30 days' notice

MODEL 03

Embedded specialists

One engineer, quality engineer or security specialist inside your team full-time.

Best when: your process works and you need capacity or a skill you cannot hire quickly.

How we keep it honest: the same person stays. If we ever need to rotate someone, you get notice and a handover, not a surprise new face in the stand-up.

From R92 000 / month

MODEL 04

Assessment & advisory

A time-boxed piece of expert work with a written deliverable.

Best when: you need to know something before you can decide anything — is this codebase salvageable, are we exposed, is this AI idea worth funding.

The deal: fixed fee, fixed duration, and the report is yours to use however you like, including as the brief for a different supplier.

From R32 000

Pricing

Why our prices are on the website.

Most firms in this market hide pricing. The reason given is that "every project is different". The real reason is that the number is set by how wealthy the buyer looks and how badly they appear to need it.

We would rather publish. It means you can budget without three weeks of phone tag, it means nobody pays a premium for sounding inexperienced on a call, and it means both of us find out quickly if our rates are wrong for your situation.

Build an instant quote

What moves the number up

  • Accelerated timeline — +18%. Moving fast means reprioritising other clients' work, and we would rather charge for it than quietly cut corners.
  • Regulated environments — additional evidence, documentation and audit support take real time.
  • Integration with systems nobody documented — the single most common cause of estimates moving.
  • A care plan — +15% of the build, annually, for patching, monitoring and a monthly health report.

What moves it down

  • Three or more disciplines together — −7%. One mobilisation instead of three genuinely costs us less.
  • A flexible timeline — −8%. Work we can schedule around other commitments is cheaper to deliver.
  • A clear brief and a decisive counterpart — the cheapest projects are the ones where somebody on your side can actually make a decision.
  • Doing discovery properly — it feels like an extra cost at the start and is almost always the cheapest part of the whole engagement.

Excluded from all published prices: third-party software licences, cloud hosting and AI model or API usage — these are billed to your own accounts rather than marked up through us. Also excluded: travel outside Gauteng, physical penetration testing, hardware, and anything outside the agreed scope. VAT is shown separately where it applies.

Working with us

House rules, both directions.

Things we commit to, and the two things we ask of you in return.

What we commit to

  • A named delivery lead who is accountable and reachable
  • A written weekly update whether or not there is good news
  • Bad news early, in full, with options — never at the deadline
  • Your repository and cloud account, never ours
  • No proprietary frameworks you would have to licence from us
  • Estimates with the assumptions written down beside them
  • An honest recommendation even when it costs us the work
  • Documentation written for a stranger, not for us

What we ask of you

  • A decision-maker. One person who can say yes or no within a couple of days. Projects do not usually fail on engineering; they stall waiting for approval.
  • Honesty about constraints. The real deadline, the real budget, the political situation, the system nobody wants to touch. We can design around almost anything we know about, and nothing we do not.

That is the whole list. Everything else is our job.
