5. How to request access to a record

  1. Complete Form C (Request for Access to Record), as prescribed in the PAIA regulations. The form is available from the Information Regulator's website.
  2. Submit the completed form to the Information Officer at the address or email in section 1 above.
  3. Provide enough detail to enable us to identify the record and to identify you. If you are requesting on behalf of someone else, attach proof of your authority.
  4. State the form of access you require and the manner in which you wish to be informed of the decision.
  5. If the request is made in order to exercise or protect a right, state which right and explain how the record is required for that purpose.
  6. Pay the prescribed request fee where applicable. We will notify you of any access fee before processing the request, and may require a deposit.

We will respond within 30 days of receiving the request. That period may be extended by a further 30 days in the circumstances set out in section 57 of PAIA, in which case we will tell you and give reasons.

6. Fees

Fees are those prescribed in the regulations published under PAIA, as amended from time to time. A requester seeking access to a record containing their own personal information is not required to pay the request fee. Access fees may still apply for reproduction, search and preparation, and postage.

7. Grounds for refusal

Access may be refused on the grounds set out in Chapter 4 of PAIA, which include:

  • Mandatory protection of the privacy of a third party who is a natural person
  • Mandatory protection of the commercial information of a third party
  • Mandatory protection of confidential information of a third party
  • Mandatory protection of the safety of individuals and of property
  • Mandatory protection of records privileged from production in legal proceedings
  • Commercial information of the private body, including trade secrets and information that could harm our commercial or financial interests
  • Requests that are manifestly frivolous or vexatious, or that would involve a substantial and unreasonable diversion of resources

Where access is refused we will give reasons and inform you of your right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator or to apply to a court.

8. Remedies

If your request is refused, or you are dissatisfied with how it was handled, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator in terms of section 77A of PAIA, or apply to a court of competent jurisdiction.

Details of the personal information we process, the purposes, the recipients, cross-border transfers and the security measures applied are set out in our privacy notice, which forms part of this manual.

10. Availability of this manual
