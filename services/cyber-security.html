The local picture

South African organisations are attractive targets and lightly defended.

The Protection of Personal Information Act has been fully enforceable since July 2021, and the Information Regulator has moved from guidance to enforcement notices. A breach is no longer only a technical embarrassment — it is a reportable event with statutory deadlines, a board-level disclosure and potential administrative fines.

At the same time, most mid-sized businesses here have never had an independent security assessment. Not because they do not care, but because the market has historically priced it as an enterprise purchase and wrapped it in jargon. We publish our prices for exactly that reason.

The report

Two audiences, one document.

Most security reports fail because they are written for one reader and handed to another. Ours are built for both — an executive section your board can act on in ten minutes, and a technical section your developers can work from without a translator.

You also get the raw evidence: requests, responses, screenshots and proof-of-concept detail, so that nothing rests on us simply asserting it.

For the board

Business risk in plain language, a single overall posture rating, the three things that matter most, what they would cost to fix, and the regulatory exposure if they are not.

For the engineers

Each finding with CVSS v4 severity, affected component, full reproduction steps, evidence, and a specific remediation — not "implement input validation".

For procurement

A clean attestation letter suitable for sharing with your own customers, auditors or insurers, confirming scope, dates, methodology and outcome.

For the next test

A tracked findings register carried forward, so the next assessment measures progress instead of starting again from zero.

Questions

Security FAQs.

A vulnerability assessment is largely automated. It finds known weaknesses — missing patches, weak configuration, exposed services — quickly and cheaply across a wide surface. A penetration test is a person actively trying to break in, chaining small weaknesses together and attacking the business logic that no scanner understands.

Scanners will never find "any logged-in user can change the account number in the URL and see someone else's statement". A tester will. If budget is tight, start with the assessment; if you handle money or personal information, you need the test.

Testing is designed not to. We agree the rules of engagement in advance, exclude anything genuinely fragile, avoid destructive techniques and denial-of-service by default, and run intensive activity in an agreed window. There is a named contact on both sides and we stop immediately on request. Where possible we test a staging environment that mirrors production.

Annually as a baseline, and additionally after any significant architectural change, a new payment or authentication flow, a cloud migration, or a merger. Continuous monitoring fills the gaps between tests far more cheaply than testing repeatedly, which is why we offer it as a retainer rather than pushing four penetration tests a year at you.
