About

A small firm that does serious work.

Bulan is a founder-led engineering studio in Johannesburg. We build software, prove it works, defend it against people who would rather it did not, and automate the work around it.

The name

Bulan means moon.

It is the word for moon in Malay and Indonesian, and we chose it for what the moon does rather than what it is. It shows up reliably. It works through the night. It is the thing that keeps the tides predictable — quiet infrastructure that everything else depends on and nobody thinks about until it stops.

That is the kind of software we want to be known for. Not the demo that gets applause in a boardroom. The system that is still running correctly at two in the morning on the last Friday of the month when payroll goes out.

Why we started

We got tired of watching the same project fail the same way.

Between us we have spent years inside South African software delivery — on the build side, the quality side and the security side. The failure pattern barely changes. A system is built to a deadline. Testing is compressed into the last two weeks. Security is a penetration test six months after launch, from a different supplier, whose report sits in a shared drive because nobody has capacity to act on it.

Then something breaks in production, and three companies explain why it is not their fault.

Bulan is our attempt at the obvious alternative: one team that is accountable for all three, small enough that the people who sold you the work are the people doing it, and transparent enough that you never have to guess what something costs or how it is going.

We are new. We are not hiding that, and we have put four structural protections in place so that betting on a new firm does not require faith.

What we believe

Six opinions we will not apologise for.

01

Boring technology wins

We choose stacks with deep local hiring pools and long support horizons. Your system should not become unmaintainable because we enjoyed a framework in 2026.

02

Estimates are ranges with assumptions

Anyone giving you a single confident number for complex work is either padding it or guessing. We show the range and write the assumptions down beside it.

03

Security is a build decision

A penetration test after launch finds what you built wrong. Threat modelling before the first commit stops you building it wrong. The second is dramatically cheaper.

04

The handover is the product

Software you cannot maintain without us is not an asset, it is a subscription you did not agree to. We write documentation for a stranger.

05

Say no early

Turning down work we should not take costs us a month of revenue. Taking it costs us a reputation. We are not confused about which is worse.

06

Built here, sold everywhere

South African engineering is world-class and consistently undersold, usually by South Africans. We price fairly and compete on standards, not on being cheap.
