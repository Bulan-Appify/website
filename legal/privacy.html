5. Cross-border transfers

Some of the services above store data outside South Africa. Section 72 of POPIA permits such transfers only in defined circumstances — principally where the recipient is subject to a law or binding agreement providing an adequate level of protection, or where the transfer is necessary to perform a contract with you.

Where we transfer personal information outside South Africa we rely on contractual safeguards with those providers. You may ask us which providers are involved and where they store data, and we will tell you.

7. How we protect it

We apply the technical and organisational measures required by section 19 of POPIA, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS across the whole site) and at rest in our systems
  • Multi-factor authentication on every business account
  • Least-privilege access, reviewed periodically
  • Written operator agreements with the suppliers listed above
  • A documented breach response procedure
  • Collecting as little as possible in the first place, which is the most effective control available

No system is perfectly secure. We would rather say that plainly than imply a guarantee nobody can give.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Be told whether we hold personal information about you, and to receive a copy of it
  • Request correction or deletion of information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained
  • Object, on reasonable grounds, to our processing of your information
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis we rely on
  • Object to direct marketing at any time
  • Complain to the Information Regulator

To exercise any of these, email privacy@bulan.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable period and in any event as required by PAIA. Formal access requests follow the process in our PAIA manual.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website sets no tracking cookies, loads no third-party fonts, no advertising pixels and no social media trackers. See our cookie notice for the full position.

10. Personal information we process for clients

When we deliver services, we frequently process personal information that belongs to our clients' data subjects — for example while testing a system or investigating an incident. In that situation our client is the responsible party and Bulan is an operator under section 21 of POPIA.

In that role we:

  • Sign an operator agreement before processing begins
  • Process only on the client's documented instruction
  • Use synthetic or irreversibly anonymised data for testing wherever it is possible, rather than copies of live data
  • Notify the client immediately where we have reasonable grounds to believe information has been accessed by an unauthorised person
  • Return or securely destroy the information at the end of the engagement

If you are a data subject of one of our clients, please direct your request to that organisation, since they determine why and how their information is processed. We will assist them in responding to you.

11. Security compromises

If personal information in our care is accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify the Information Regulator and the affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible after establishing that a compromise occurred, as required by section 22 of POPIA. The notification will describe what happened, the likely consequences, and what we and you can do about it.

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in one of our systems, please see our vulnerability disclosure policy.

12. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our practices change. The version number and date at the top will change with it. Material changes affecting people whose information we already hold will be communicated directly where we have a means of doing so.
