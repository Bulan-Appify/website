Why together

The seams between suppliers are where projects fail.

When the builder, the tester and the security assessor are three different companies, every defect becomes a negotiation. The build team says it is a test environment issue. The test team says it is a requirements issue. The security team files a finding and disappears. Nobody is wrong, the problem stays open, and you pay for all three to argue.

Combining the disciplines does not just save money — although it does, and the quote builder applies a 7% discount when you take three or more. It removes the seam. One delivery lead is accountable for whether the thing works, is proven and is defensible.
