Where we work

Sectors where getting it wrong is expensive.

We are not going to claim deep expertise in every industry on earth. These are the environments whose constraints we understand — regulatory pressure, integration debt, uneven connectivity and real consequences when a system is wrong.

01

Financial services & fintech

Payments, lending, onboarding, collections and the reporting that surrounds them. High regulatory load, low tolerance for downtime, and an attack surface that is actively probed.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • Penetration testing before a payment or authentication change ships
  • Integration layers between core banking and everything else
  • Performance testing for month-end and pay-day load
  • Document intelligence for onboarding and FICA verification

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

POPIAFICAPCI DSS scopeAudit evidenceData residency

02

Healthcare

Practice management, patient portals, claims and medical scheme integration. Personal health information is special personal information under POPIA, which changes what you may lawfully do with it.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • POPIA readiness where special personal information is processed
  • Secure patient-facing portals and appointment systems
  • Integration with practice and medical scheme systems
  • Access control review — who can see which record, and why

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

POPIA s26 special PIHPCSA guidanceRetention rulesConsent trails

03

Retail & e-commerce

Storefronts, stock, pricing, fulfilment and loyalty. The interesting engineering problem is not the shop — it is the four systems behind it that disagree about how much stock exists.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • Load and soak testing ahead of Black Friday and festive peak
  • Stock, pricing and order integration across channels
  • Payment gateway integration and checkout conversion work
  • Support automation for the queries that repeat endlessly

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

Peak tradingCPAPayment securityMobile-first traffic

04

Logistics & supply chain

Tracking, scheduling, proof of delivery and integration across partners who all standardised on something different. Frequently offline, frequently on a phone, frequently in a place with no signal.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • Mobile applications that work properly offline and sync later
  • Partner and customer integration — EDI, API and, still, CSV
  • Operational dashboards that reflect reality, not yesterday
  • Route, load and scheduling automation

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

Offline-firstLow bandwidthDevice fleetCross-border data

05

Mining & industrial

Compliance reporting, safety data capture, contractor management and the long, awkward bridge between operational technology and the corporate network.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • Network segmentation review between OT and IT environments
  • Digitised safety, incident and inspection capture
  • Compliance and production reporting automation
  • Contractor and access management systems

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

MHSA reportingOT/IT boundaryRemote sitesRuggedised devices

06

Public sector & NGOs

Citizen-facing services, grant and beneficiary systems, and donor reporting. Accessibility and low-bandwidth performance are not nice-to-haves here — they determine whether people can use the service at all.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • Accessibility audits and remediation to WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Services that work on a feature phone budget of data
  • Beneficiary and grant management systems
  • Independent quality assurance on supplier-delivered systems

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

PFMA / MFMASITA processesB-BBEE scoringAccessibility

07

Education

Learning platforms, student administration and assessment. When learners are minors, POPIA's consent rules change materially — and most institutions have not adjusted for it.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • Student and learner management systems
  • POPIA review where personal information of children is processed
  • Assessment platform integrity and load testing
  • Accessibility for learners using assistive technology

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

POPIA s34–35 childrenExam-period loadDevice diversity

08

Professional services

Law, accounting, audit, engineering and consulting firms: document-heavy, deadline-driven, and sitting on more confidential client information than most banks.

WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO

  • Practice management and client portal development
  • Document intelligence and matter automation
  • Internal assistants grounded in the firm's own precedent
  • Security assessment — you are a soft route to your clients

CONSTRAINTS WE PLAN FOR

Privilege & confidentialityConflict checksRetention obligations

Honest caveat

Your sector is not on this list.

That is usually fine. Domain knowledge matters enormously, but it lives with you — you know your business better than any consultant will after six weeks of interviews. What we bring is the engineering, the testing discipline and the security judgement, plus enough curiosity to learn your domain properly rather than pretending we already have.

Where we genuinely should not take the work — a specialist regulatory environment we do not understand, or something needing accreditation we do not hold — we will say so and try to point you somewhere better.

Tell us about your environment.

The constraints usually matter more than the requirements. Start there and we will tell you what we would do about it.
