The problem

Most vendors hand you the build and wish you luck.

The software gets delivered. Then the bugs surface in production, the penetration test lands three months later from a different supplier, and nobody can say whose fault it is. Meanwhile the invoices keep arriving from three directions.

Bulan exists because building, proving and defending software should not be three separate procurement exercises. We do all three under one contract, with one team that cannot pass the blame sideways.
